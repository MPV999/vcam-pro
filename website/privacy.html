Privacy policy

VCAM Pro runs entirely on your device. This policy describes exactly what the app does with data, and what it does not do.

What the app stores

  • Photos and videos you capture, and files you import, inside the app's private storage (/data/user/0/com.vcam.pro/files/media).
  • Metadata about those files (name, size, resolution, duration) in a local Room database.
  • Your preferences (theme, language, default pipeline settings) in a local DataStore file.
  • Virtual camera test sessions: source label, frame counts, measured FPS.

What leaves the device

Nothing, unless you explicitly export a file through MediaStore. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, no account system and no server component. It does not request the INTERNET permission for its own use.

Permissions

  • Camera — to capture photos and videos and to use a physical camera as a virtual camera source.
  • Microphone — only when you record a video with sound.
  • Storage Access Framework — read access only to the single file you pick.

What the app never does

  • It does not read your gallery, contacts, messages or other apps' data.
  • It does not replace or modify the system camera for other applications.
  • It does not hook, patch or instrument third-party processes.
  • It is not intended to bypass identity verification, liveness detection or KYC systems.

Deleting data

Deleting an item in the library removes the database row and the file. Uninstalling the app removes everything, because nothing is stored anywhere else.
